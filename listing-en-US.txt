APP NAME (30): Knockout Zoo: Arcade Boxing

SHORT DESCRIPTION (80): Pick your animal. Throw your hands. 15 wild fighters, 17 crazy fights.

FULL DESCRIPTION:
HUMANS VS. BEASTS. PICK YOUR ANIMAL. THROW YOUR HANDS.

Knockout Zoo is fast, loud, arcade boxing the way it used to be: easy to pick up, hard to put down, and way more ridiculous. Step into the ring as a human brawler or a wild animal and punch your way through a 17-fight circuit to become champ.

15 FIGHTERS, ZERO CHILL
Rocky Ray. Big Mo. Tina Thunder. Billy Bash the goat. Roo Rumble the kangaroo. Grizzly Gabe. Gorilla Grimm. Chimp Chucky. Ozzy Ostrich. Gator Gus. Hugo Hippo. And more. Every fighter has their own style.

ARENAS THAT FIGHT BACK
Box in a backyard gym, a county fair barn, a jungle clearing, on a skyscraper roof, in a steel cage, on a frozen rink, over a lava pit, on an orbital space station, in a deep-sea dome and finally in the championship ring. Some ring edges burn, ice is slippery, and underwater everything moves in slow motion.

SIMPLE CONTROLS, BIG HITS
Step, Jab, Hook, Block. That's it. Chain jabs into combos, land a heavy hook, and feel every hit with screen shake and slow-mo knockdowns. Three rounds per fight. Two knockdowns is a KO.

GLOVES WITH SUPERPOWERS
Earn purses every fight and spend them in the Glove Shop: Steel Knuckle, Frostbite (freezes), Fireball (burns), Thunder Volt (stuns) and the legendary Golden Champ. Grab outfits in the Locker for extra HP.

PLAY YOUR FRIENDS
Fight a friend online with a 4-letter code or a share link. Or throw a party tournament: up to 8 players join one link, the game makes the bracket, and the last one standing is champ. No chat, no strangers.

FREE TO START, NO ADS EVER
The first 5 fights and online play are free. One $3.99 purchase unlocks the whole circuit. No ads, no subscriptions, no coins for sale. Purchases sit behind a grown-up check.

MADE BY A FAMILY, FOR FAMILIES
Knockout Zoo was built by a dad with his kids as the official testers. Cartoon action, no blood, and a game parents can actually play with their kids.

Get in the ring. Who's your champ?
