Privacy Policy

BIRCO, Inc. · Effective October 22, 2026

This policy covers the BIRCO Arcade driving game, on the web and in its Android and iPhone apps. The game is made for players of all ages, including children. It is free and has no ads and no in-app purchases.

What we collect

Nothing. The game has no accounts, no sign-in, no ads, no analytics and no tracking. We do not ask for a name, email, location, photos, contacts or any other personal information.

What stays on your device

Game progress, coins, garage upgrades and settings are saved only on your own device. Deleting the app (or clearing the browser's site data) removes them. We never see this data.

Online VS mode

Online VS lets friends play together by sharing a private 4-letter room code. There is no chat and no way to find or contact strangers. To connect the phones, the game uses PeerJS, a free public connection service. During a match, PeerJS briefly handles the room code and each device's network address so the devices can reach each other; the game data then travels directly between the players' devices. We do not store any of it. Players who never use Online VS never connect to it.

Children

Because we collect no personal information, we collect none from children under 13. Parents can contact us with any question.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and its effective date.
