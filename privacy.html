Privacy Policy

BIRCO, Inc. · Effective November 15, 2026

This policy covers the BIRCO Arcade driving game (Blast Buggies), on the web and in its Android and iPhone apps. The web game is free with no ads. The apps are free to start, offer one optional Full Game purchase, and offer optional reward videos that a player chooses to watch.

What we collect

We do not collect anything ourselves. The game has no accounts, no sign-in and no analytics, and we never ask for a name, email, location, photos, contacts or any other personal information.

Reward videos in the apps (Google AdMob)

In the Android and iPhone apps a player can choose to watch a short video to double the cash from a run. Videos never play on their own and never during play. They are served by Google AdMob, which may collect a device advertising ID, IP address and basic device and app-usage information to show and measure ads and to prevent fraud, as described in Google's advertising policy. Ads are limited to family-friendly (G-rated) content. Where the law requires it (for example in the EEA and UK), the app asks for consent first and offers a "Privacy choices" button to change it. On iPhone, Apple's tracking prompt lets you say no; reward videos still work. Owners of the Full Game never see ads.

Full Game purchase

The optional Full Game purchase is handled entirely by Google Play or the App Store. We never see your payment details. The app only checks with the store whether the purchase exists, so it can unlock the game and restore it on a new phone.

More from BIRCO

The game shows a list of other BIRCO games and Recon the Raccoon videos. It downloads that list from our website. Tapping one opens the store or YouTube; the link includes a tag that names this game, so we can count how many players found the other game through it. No personal information is sent.

What stays on your device

Game progress, coins, garage upgrades and settings are saved only on your own device. Deleting the app (or clearing the browser's site data) removes them. We never see this data.

Online VS mode

Online VS lets friends play together by sharing a private 4-letter room code. There is no chat and no way to find or contact strangers. To connect the phones, the game uses PeerJS, a free public connection service. During a match, PeerJS briefly handles the room code and each device's network address so the devices can reach each other; the game data then travels directly between the players' devices. We do not store any of it. Players who never use Online VS never connect to it.

Children

We collect no personal information ourselves. The apps are not directed at children under 13; ads are limited to G-rated content, and purchases are behind a grown-up check. Parents can contact us with any question.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and its effective date.
