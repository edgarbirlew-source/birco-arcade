Knockout Zoo Support

Need help? Email BIRCO, Inc. at edgarb@bircoinc.com and we'll answer within two business days.

I bought the Full Game on another phone

Tap FULL GAME on the fight map, then Restore purchase. Use the same Apple ID or Google account you bought it with.

My friend can't join my online fight

Both phones need the internet. Tap TEXT A FRIEND to send the invite, or read your 4-letter code out loud and have your friend type it under Join. Codes only work while the first player is waiting.

How do I play?

Tap or use the on-screen buttons to jab, hook and block. Win fights to unlock new animals, gloves and arenas.
