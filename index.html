HOW TO PLAY
  • Street run: drive the road, blast the bad guys ahead, dodge air strikes (red target circles) and obstacles. Hit the goal, then cross the finish line
  • VS Arena: free-drive a City, Town, Demolition Derby or Harbor. Hunt your rivals before they hunt you. Last one standing wins
  • Street: drag the road or slider to steer. Arena: push the joystick toward where you want to go (it starts wherever your thumb lands), or drag on the screen. Stuck on a wall? Keep pushing and you back out
  • FIRE guns · FLAME torches up close · BOMB lobs a blast · DEPLOY sends a drone: near rivals it fights beside you, far away it flies off as a flying bomb, with nobody around it adds gun power
  • Pickups: 💣 bombs · 🔧 repair · 🛡️ shield · ⛽ fuel · 🛸 drone · ⚡ boost · ✈️ pads give you jet wings
  • Keys: arrows steer (arena: ↑ gas, ↓ reverse), Space fire, F flame, B bomb, E drone, V view
