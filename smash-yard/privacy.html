Privacy Policy

BIRCO, Inc. · Effective November 15, 2026

This policy covers Smash Yard, a brawler game made for players of all ages, including children. It covers the Android and iPhone apps and the web version. Smash Yard has no ads, no analytics and no tracking.

The Android and iPhone apps

We collect nothing. The apps have no accounts, no sign-in and no chat. We never ask for a name, email, location, photos or contacts.

Game progress, coins, skins and settings are saved only on your own device. Deleting the app removes them.

Online 1v1: one player makes a private room and the other types its 4-letter code. During the match, moves travel through BIRCO's game server and are passed straight to the other player. Nothing is stored after the room closes. There is no chat and no way to find or contact strangers.

Full Game purchase: purchases are handled by Google Play or the App Store. BIRCO never sees payment details. The buy button sits behind a grown-up check.

The web version (optional accounts)

On the web version a player may make an account to add friends. We keep only a username (3 to 12 letters or numbers, checked for bad words) and a one-way scrambled code made from the email address. The email itself is never stored or shown to anyone. Friends find each other by username only. There is no chat. To delete a web account, email us from a parent's email and we will remove it within 30 days.

Children

The apps collect no personal information from anyone, including children under 13. Parents can contact us with any question or request.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and its date.
